## Reading the numbers

- **Similarity**: 1 − character error rate between what the player would speak and the expected text, after folding whitespace, Unicode normalization, soft hyphens and zero-width characters. Order counts: interleaved columns cost about twice their length.
- **Word recall**: share of expected words that are spoken. Low means text is lost or garbled.
- **Word precision**: share of spoken words that are expected. Low means clutter is read aloud (running headers, page numbers, figure labels) or words are garbled.
- **Expected text**: `exact` comes from the generator that made the PDF; `draft` was corrected by eye and waits for a hand-check; `checked` was hand-checked. Rules: `pdf/golden/README.md`.
- Not scored here: scanned pages (OCR runs on the phone, P3-05), real legacy-font PDFs (not redistributable; the legacy categories are generated, ADR-023) and password or damaged files (device tests).
