# Made shows covers or a list with icon buttons, and its player looks like the book player (2026-10-28, ADR 2026-10-28-made-tab)
- Made: the books' covers in a grid or the library's rows (one grid/list choice for both places, toggled in Made's top
  bar), each with Play or Open, Share and ⋮ (Delete) as icon buttons, the kind on the cover, kind filter chips, and
  Jobs as a chip in the top bar saying how many are making.
- The saved-audio player: the book's cover, chapter name and save details, a seek bar with chapter n of N and time
  left, and the book player's controls panel with 15 s and 30 s skips, Speed (the book player's Voice and speed sheet
  with a note that the voice is in the file) and the book player's sleep timer, kept by the saved-audio service.
- "What's new" entry: `app/src/main/assets/whatsnew/2026-10-28-made-tab.txt`. `:pdf` unchanged. No new dependency.
- **Cost:** none at rest. The grid reuses the library's cover cache; the timer is one Handler post or one player flag.
  APK a few KB.
- **Could affect:** the library's grid/list choice (now shared with Made), the book player's sleep timer button and
  speed control (now shared), a book's details sheet (its made files show as rows), Jobs (now a top bar chip).
- **Verified in CI:** `MadeSleepTimerTest` (minutes, chapter ends, off), `MadeAndEditScreensTest` (grid plays from its
  button and opens Jobs, list toggle sets the layout, kind chips filter, empty, player panel and Speed sheet, sleep
  choices without End of page, chapters sheet), release journey steps 19 (Jobs chip) and 32 (a saved audiobook plays
  with the speed sheet and the sleep timer set and cleared through the service), the UI tour.

**Real-device checks (the test phone):** save a book as an audiobook; open Made: covers, two across; switch to list in
the top bar and check the library is a list too; play; set Speed to 1.5×; set the sleep timer to 15 minutes, turn the
screen off and check it pauses; set End of chapter on a many-chapter audiobook; Delete from ⋮; TalkBack names each
button with its book.
