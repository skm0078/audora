# Swipe or tap the edge to turn a PDF page; the pages beside are drawn ahead (2026-10-28, ADR 2026-10-28-page-flip)
- A sideways swipe on a PDF page turns it, with the next page sliding in under the finger; a tap in the outer fifth
  of the page (away from a sentence) turns it too. The next page and the one before are drawn ahead, so a turn lands
  on a finished page. "Read from here" appears beside "Back to page N" after turning away by hand.
- "What's new" entry: `app/src/main/assets/whatsnew/2026-10-28-page-flip.txt`. `:pdf` unchanged. No new dependency.
- **Cost:** about 13 MB more memory while a PDF page is shown (two page bitmaps), none zoomed in or in the text view;
  lighter option `PageFlipConfig(drawPrevious = false)` (about 6.6 MB). APK about 10 KB. Cold start: none.
- **Could affect:** the page view's pinch zoom and pan, tap to read, long-press menus, the page scrubber's thumbnails
  (they share the render lock), following the reading position across pages.
- **Verified in CI:** `PageFlipConfigTest` (swipe and edge rules), `PageNeighboursTest` (what is held),
  `PageFlipModelTest` (drawn ahead, instant turn, three pages at most, zoom and text view, edge taps),
  `ReaderPageViewTest` (updated render counts), `PageFlipTest` on the emulator (swipe turns, nudge and first page
  do not, Read from here in Hindi and Bengali at 2x, screenshots), release journey step 20.

**Real-device checks (the test phone):** swipe through ten pages of a PDF quickly, forwards and back: no spinner
after the first; pinch to zoom and pan sideways: no page turn; tap a line: it reads; tap the page's far right margin:
next page; start reading, swipe two pages on, tap "Read from here": reading moves to that page.
