# Pages curl as they turn (2026-10-29, ADR 2026-10-29-page-curl)
- A page now curls like paper when it turns, in a PDF and in the text view with Turn pages on: a finger lifts the
  corner it took (top or bottom third) or the whole edge (middle third), with a shadow at the fold and the page's
  back showing faintly. Reading aloud and the arrows curl the page by themselves (250 ms while reading). Page and
  text offers Curl (default), Slide or None, Quick, Normal or Slow, and a light or deep shadow.
- Turn pages is on by default for text books (`turn_pages_v2`, so a reader who never touched it gets the new
  default). `PageCurl` (`:core`), `FlipState`, `FlipScenes`, `CurlFrame`, `PageTurn`, `TextTurns`. What's new entry
  `2026-10-29-page-curl`. `:pdf` not changed (PIPELINE_VERSION unchanged). No new dependency.
- **Could affect:** PDF swipes and arrow turns, the text view's turns and reading-on, Page and text sheet.
- **Verified in CI:** `PageCurlTest` (`:core`: fold, flat, flap, spine reach, turning path), `PageCurlTest`
  (device: drag curl with shadow, slide without, corner drag, reading auto-curl), `TextPagesTest` (a curl over the
  text never moves it), `PageTurnTest`, `PageFlipConfigTest`, release journey step 50.

**Cost:** PDF none more (the neighbour pages are already drawn); text view one picture of the text area during a
turn (about 8 MB GPU, 4 MB CPU fallback), freed after; three fills per frame while turning; about 20 KB APK; nothing
at cold start. Slide or None in Page and text are the lighter options.

**Real-device checks (the test phone):** curl a PDF page slowly from the bottom corner and from the middle; let
reading run over a page end; turn a text book page back and forth; try Slide and None.
