1. **Set bedtime once.** In the reader, tap the timer icon, then **Sleep options…**. Turn on
   **Set a timer by itself at bedtime** and set **Bedtime starts** and **Bedtime ends**. From now on, a timer starts by
   itself whenever you listen at night.
2. **Let it fade.** In the same sheet, turn on **Fade the voice out at the end**, so the voice drifts off instead of
   stopping mid-word. Turn on **Shake the phone to restart the timer** too, for the nights you are still awake.
   Tap **Save**.
3. **Add a soft background.** Tap the speed button and, under **Sound**, pick a **Focus sound** such as **Rain** or
   **Fireplace**. It plays quietly under the voice and gets softer whenever the voice speaks.
4. **Press play and put the phone down.** Lock it. The book reads until the timer ends.
5. **Tomorrow, just press play.** ListenLock starts again at the same sentence, even after a restart.

## Why it works

The bedtime timer means you never have to remember to set one. The fade and the shake-to-restart cover the two
annoying moments of sleep listening: being woken by a sudden stop, and drifting off before you meant to.

## If it goes wrong

If you missed the last part, scroll back a page in the reader and press and hold the sentence to start from there.
If reading stops before the timer ends with the screen off, your phone may be stopping background apps: search the
Library for **Background reading**, which shows the setting for your phone's brand.
