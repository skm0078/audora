1. **Add the textbook.** In the Library, tap **Add book** and pick the PDF. If its pages are scans, the reader says the
   page is scanned and offers the text recognition files: tap **Download** once. They cover English, Hindi and Bengali.
2. **Prepare tomorrow's chapter.** Back in the Library, press and hold the book, then tap **Studio mode**. It prepares
   the speech ahead of time with your voice and speed. Plug the phone in and leave it overnight.
3. **Listen on the way.** Open the book, press play and lock the phone. Tap the timer icon and choose
   **End of chapter**, so reading stops at a natural break instead of mid-paragraph.
4. **Mark what matters.** When a line is worth keeping, tap **Bookmark this sentence** in the player. Later, in the
   reader, press and hold that sentence and tap **Highlight**.
5. **Revise before the exam.** Tap the speed button, open **What to read** and turn on **Skim**: you hear only
   headings, captions, the first sentence of each paragraph and sentences with bold terms.
6. **Test yourself.** In the Library, tap ⋮ and then **Quiz me**. Each highlight becomes a question with one word left
   out, read aloud.

## Why it works

Scanned pages are slow to recognise and the best voices are slow to render. Studio mode does both while you sleep, so
nothing stutters on a crowded bus. The chapter timer matches how textbooks are split, and your highlights turn into
revision without any extra writing.

## If it goes wrong

The reader marks each scanned page with its recognition quality. If a page says **poor**, the scan is faint or
crooked. Take a fresh photo of that page in good light with **Point and read** (Library ⋮). If a sentence sounds
wrong, press and hold it and tap **This sounded wrong**, so the problem is noted with where it happened.
