1. **Use TalkBack as usual.** Every screen in ListenLock has labels, headings and actions for TalkBack, and rows stay
   readable at Android's largest text sizes. Search in the Library finds books, actions and settings by name, which
   is often quicker than exploring menus.
2. **Make the page easier to see.** In the reader, tap ⋮ and then **Page and text**. Turn on **Focus view** for larger
   text with everything but the sentence being read dimmed, and **Highlight each word** to follow along word by word.
3. **Pick letters that are easier to read.** In **Page and text**, under **Font**, try **Easy letters (Atkinson
   Hyperlegible)** or **Dyslexia-friendly (OpenDyslexic)**. These change English text only; Hindi and Bengali keep
   their usual font.
4. **Control reading by voice.** Search the Library for **Voice commands**, download the model, then turn on
   **Listen for voice commands** and **Keep listening with the screen off**. Say “ListenLock, pause”, “play”, “next”,
   “back”, “repeat”, “faster” or “slower”.
5. **Keep your hands free.** Headset buttons and the lock screen controls play, pause and skip, and ListenLock resumes
   at the same sentence every time.

## Why it works

TalkBack handles the app, ListenLock's own voice handles the book, and voice commands mean you do not need to find
the screen at all once a book is playing. Everything is heard on the phone; nothing is recorded or sent.

## If it goes wrong

If TalkBack and the book voice talk over each other, pause the book before exploring the screen, or use a voice
command instead of the screen. If a control is hard to find or has a confusing label,
please [tell us](https://github.com/skm0078/audora/issues) which screen it was.
