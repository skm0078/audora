## Reading the numbers

- **Similarity**: 1 − character error rate between the heard words and the script's words. Only words count: case, punctuation and spacing are ignored (the listener hears them as pauses).
- **Word recall**: share of the script's words that are heard. Low means text skipped or garbled.
- **Word precision**: share of heard words that are in the script. Low means junk, footnote markers, symbol soup or table cells are read aloud.
- **Script**: `draft` was written from the page and corrected by eye; `checked` was hand-checked by the maintainer. How scripts are written: `pdf/golden/listening/README.md`.
