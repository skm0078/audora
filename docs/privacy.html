Audora privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Audora is a free, open-source Android app that reads PDFs aloud. It is published on Google Play by the developer TelosMind. In short: Audora does not collect, store on any server, or share any information about you. Everything it does happens on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • The PDFs you open, their text, and any pages read with OCR (text recognition).
  • Your library, reading positions, bookmarks, notes and settings.
  • Audio from the microphone, only when you use a feature that listens: voice commands and practice features turn speech into text on the phone and keep no recording; a voice note you record is saved in the app on your phone until you delete it. No audio is ever sent anywhere.
  • The app's own logs. They leave the phone only if you choose Share logs in Settings and pick where to send them.

Audora has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting service. Backups and exports are files you create and save where you choose.

What Audora downloads

When you choose to download a voice, a text-recognition model or another language pack, Audora fetches the file over HTTPS from GitHub (github.com) or Hugging Face (huggingface.co). These requests send no information about you or your documents. Like any web request, the server sees your IP address, under GitHub's and Hugging Face's own privacy policies.

Google Play

If you install Audora from Google Play, Google may share anonymous install statistics and crash reports with the developer, as it does for every Play app, under Google's privacy policy. These never include your documents.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the playback controls and the progress of long jobs.
  • Microphone: only for voice commands, voice notes and listening features you use.
  • Internet: only for the downloads described above.

Children

Audora is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date. The full history is public in the source repository. Questions: open an issue at github.com/skm0078/audora/issues.
