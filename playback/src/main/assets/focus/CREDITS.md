# Focus sound recordings

Every loop here is cut from a field recording released on Freesound under **Creative Commons 0** (public domain
dedication: free for any use, commercial included, with no attribution required). We credit the recordists anyway.
Each licence was read on the recording's own Freesound page on 2026-10-26. Recordings with people's voices in them
were left out on purpose, so no one's voice ships in the app.

Loops are trimmed, cross-faded at the seam, mixed to mono and encoded as Opus; nothing else is changed.
