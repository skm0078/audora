# Voice samples (S1)

One MP3 per voice from the S1 desktop pre-run (sherpa-onnx 1.13.8, 2 threads), 48 kbit/s mono. Each file reads
every benchmark text of its language in order: first sentence, prose, formal (UDHR article 1) and the hard
sentence with numbers and abbreviations. Texts: `spikes/voices/src/main/assets/s1-voices.json`.

- `desktop-<voice id>.mp3`: voice ids, models and licences are in the catalog and in
  `research/spikes/S1-voice-benchmark/REPORT.md`.
- The Bengali samples come from the CI run; full per-text WAVs for every voice are in `s1-desktop-samples.zip` on
  the `s1-voices` release.
- Voices trained on Lessac/Blizzard 2013 data (Piper lessac, hi_IN rohan) were measured as a reference but are
  not sampled here (ADR-012).

Audio generated by the listed models; each sample is covered by its model's licence.
